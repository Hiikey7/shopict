## Accuracy and writing rules

1. **Do not invent or infer technical, commercial, or legal facts.** In particular, do not guess the model code, panel type, resolution, refresh rate, brightness, response time, ports, speakers, smart features, stand adjustments, VESA mount, color, energy rating, warranty, accessories, availability, or barcode.
2. The only facts supplied for the example above are the product name, Samsung brand, monitor product type, and the wording “27-inch FHD Display.” Treat “FHD” as the supplied display description; do not expand it into unsupported specifications. Do not present an unverified claim as a fact.
3. If an important fact is unknown, put **“UNKNOWN — confirm before publishing”** in the internal research/checklist section and leave its customer-facing product field blank. Do not put “UNKNOWN” into public copy.
4. If you have research/browser access, verify exact-model technical details from a manufacturer source or a reliable listing for the **same model number and region**. Include source URLs and clearly separate verified facts from supplied facts. Never use a similar model's specifications as a substitute.
5. Write natural, useful English for Kenyan shoppers. Use KSh for prices. Avoid keyword stuffing, fabricated urgency, unsupported “best” or “lowest price” claims, repetitive filler, and promises the store has not confirmed.
6. Make each detail consistent everywhere: product name, model, condition, categories, specs, description, SEO title, meta description, FAQs, and image descriptions.
7. Respect the product editor's behavior: category selections can include multiple relevant main categories and subcategories; the first selected subcategory is used as the displayed product category. A SKU is generated by the store after saving, so do not make one up. Product specifications are entered as one `Label: Value` pair per line. The description is one bullet per line. Search title and description are optional and can be generated by the store if left blank.
8. Use **“Samsung Essential Monitor”** as the example product name only if that is the exact name intended for the catalog. If a verified full model name is available, recommend a clear title such as `Samsung [exact model] 27-inch FHD Monitor`; do not fill in `[exact model]` unless verified.

### 7. Search engines (SEO)

Provide a recommended value for every SEO field in the product editor. Keep fields blank where automatic generation or lack of trustworthy product information makes that safer.

- **Search title (`seo_title`):** Aim for about 50–60 characters; the editor limit is 120. Use the verified brand/model, a distinguishing confirmed spec, and “Price in Kenya” when natural. Make it unique; never include an unverified model or specification.
- **Search description (`meta_description`):** Aim for about 140–158 characters; the editor limit is 320. Write a useful, unique summary for a Kenyan shopper. Do not claim a price, stock status, delivery promise, warranty, or included item unless confirmed. Prefer a description that stays accurate if the price changes.
- **Main keyword (`focus_keyword`):** One natural, specific phrase based on the exact product and Kenyan shopping intent, e.g. a verified model plus `price in Kenya`. Do not target a model number that is not known.
- **Other keywords (`secondary_keywords`):** A short comma-separated list of relevant natural variations (model, brand, product type, location/price intent) based on verified facts. No repetition or unrelated terms. These are an internal checklist; they are not a public meta-keyword ranking signal.
- **Questions & answers (`faqs`):** Use `Q:` and `A:` lines, one question and answer per pair. Include only questions the product information can answer accurately, such as display size, resolution, connectivity, or box contents **only if confirmed**. If fewer than two genuine facts are available, write `Leave blank until specifications are verified`; the store can generate automatic FAQs from saved specs.
- **Picture descriptions (`image_alts`):** Repeat the image alt-text lines from Section 6, one line per image in exact image order.
- **Barcode (`gtin` / EAN / UPC):** Enter only the verified digits printed on the product/box or a manufacturer source; it must be an 8-, 12-, 13-, or 14-digit GTIN. Otherwise `Leave blank`.
- **Maker's part number (`mpn`):** Enter only the verified manufacturer part number for the exact model. Do not confuse it with the store's SKU. Otherwise `Leave blank`.
- **Hide from Google (`noindex`):** `No` for a unique, complete, publishable product page intended to appear in search; `Yes` only when intentionally excluding it from search results, sitemap, and Shopping feed. This is separate from “Hide from store.”
- **SEO rationale:** Briefly explain the intended search intent and why the title, description, keywords, FAQs, and image descriptions are accurate and distinct.
