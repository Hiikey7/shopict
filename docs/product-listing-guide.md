# How to list a product so it ranks on Google

The demo product **"HP EliteBook 840 G8 Laptop – Intel Core i5 11th Gen, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD, 14" Full HD, Windows 11 Pro"**
(Admin → Products, marked *Hidden*) is filled in using this guide. To list a new product:

1. Open the demo (or any similar product) and click **Duplicate**.
2. Change each field using the rules below.
3. Replace the pictures, then turn off **Hide from store** and save.

The website adds the rest for you: the Google title ("… Price in Kenya"), the Google description, price, stock and
condition details for Google Shopping results, breadcrumbs, and the picture shown when the link is shared.

---

## 1. Name (the most important field)

**Formula:** `Brand + Model + Type – Key spec, Key spec, Key spec, Key spec`

| Product   | Example name |
|-----------|--------------|
| Laptop    | HP EliteBook 840 G8 Laptop – Intel Core i5 11th Gen, 16GB RAM, 512GB SSD, 14" Full HD, Windows 11 Pro |
| Monitor   | Samsung 27" Essential Monitor S30GD – Full HD IPS, 100Hz, HDMI, Eye Saver Mode |
| Phone     | Samsung Galaxy A16 – 128GB, 4GB RAM, 6.7" Super AMOLED, 50MP Camera, 5000mAh |
| Printer   | HP LaserJet Pro MFP 4103fdw – Print, Scan, Copy, Fax, Wi-Fi, Duplex |
| Earbuds   | JBL Tune Buds Wireless Earbuds – Noise Cancelling, 48H Battery, Bluetooth 5.3 |

- Put the words people type into Google **before the dash**: brand, model number and product type.
  The site makes the Google title "HP EliteBook 840 G8 Laptop **Price in Kenya** – …" from this part.
- After the dash, list 3–5 specs buyers compare: processor, RAM, storage, screen size, camera, battery.
- Write the model number exactly as the maker does (840 G8, S30GD, A16), with no extra symbols.
- Add **Ex-UK** or **Refurbished** to the name when it isn't new. Google is then told the right condition.
- Keep it under about 120 characters, without "BEST", "CHEAP" or ALL CAPS.

## 2. Brand, categories and price

- **Brand:** always choose one. It appears on Google and in the shop filters.
- **Categories:** tick every one that fits (e.g. Laptops → Business Laptops *and* Brand New Laptops).
  More correct categories means more pages where the product is listed.
- **Old price:** set it when there's a real discount. Google and the product page then show the saving.
- **Variants:** use them for versions of the same model (Core i5 / Core i7, 128GB / 256GB) instead of creating a
  second product. Two near-identical pages compete with each other on Google.

## 3. About this item (one point per line, 6–10 lines)

Each line becomes a bullet. Start a line with the benefit, then the spec that delivers it.

1. **Opening line:** what it is, who it's for, and "in Kenya" / "Nairobi", e.g.
   *Buy the HP EliteBook 840 G8 business laptop in Kenya from Shop ICT Gadgets, Nairobi – a thin, light 14-inch laptop for office work, students and remote work.*
2. **Performance:** processor (full model, speed), RAM, and what it handles (Office, Zoom, accounting, gaming).
3. **Storage:** size and type (NVMe SSD, eMMC, HDD) and what it means (fast start-up, space for files).
4. **Screen:** size, resolution (Full HD 1920 × 1080), panel (IPS, AMOLED), refresh rate, touch.
5. **Camera / audio / graphics** (when relevant).
6. **Battery and charging:** mAh or Wh, fast charging, hours of use.
7. **Connectivity and ports:** USB-C, Thunderbolt, HDMI, Wi-Fi 6, Bluetooth version, 5G, SIM slots.
8. **Ideal for:** students, offices, gamers, designers, schools, small businesses.
9. **In the box and warranty.**
10. **Closing line:** *Free pickup at Laxmi Plaza, Nairobi or countrywide delivery to Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret and all of Kenya. Order online or on WhatsApp.*

Write naturally. Use each key phrase once or twice, not stuffed. Google ranks pages that answer the buyer's questions.

## 4. Specifications (one per line, `Name: value`)

Copy from the maker's spec sheet and keep the same names on every product of the same type, so shoppers can compare
and the Product finder matches them.

**Laptops:** Model · Processor · Graphics · RAM · Storage · Display · Operating system · Keyboard · Security ·
Ports · Wireless · Webcam · Battery · Weight · Colour · Condition · In the box

**Monitors:** Model · Screen size · Resolution · Panel type · Refresh rate · Response time · Ports · Stand ·
VESA mount · Colour · Condition

**Phones and tablets:** Model · Display · Processor · RAM · Storage · Rear camera · Front camera · Battery ·
Charging · SIM · Network · Operating system · Colour · Condition

**Printers:** Model · Functions · Print technology · Print speed · Resolution · Connectivity · Duplex ·
Paper size · Ink / toner · Condition

**Audio:** Model · Type · Connectivity · Battery life · Noise cancelling · Microphone · Water resistance ·
Colour · Condition

## 5. Pictures

- First picture: the product on a white or transparent background (it's the main image and Google's picture).
- Second picture: another angle (shown when the cursor is over the product).
- Add 3–6 more: ports, keyboard, screen on, in the box, size comparison.
- Square pictures, at least 800 × 800 px. The site makes them light automatically.

## Checklist before turning off "Hide from store"

- [ ] Name follows *Brand Model Type – key specs*
- [ ] Brand chosen, all fitting categories ticked
- [ ] Price (and old price if discounted) correct; variants for each version
- [ ] 6–10 lines in About this item, opening and closing lines included
- [ ] Specifications in `Name: value` lines
- [ ] At least 3 pictures, the first on a white background
- [ ] Specs checked against the maker's website

## Search engines (SEO) card

At the bottom of the product editor, the **Search engines (SEO)** card shows:

- how the product may look on Google;
- a checklist with a score (our own checklist, not a Google ranking);
- the search title, search description, keywords, questions and picture descriptions.

Leave those fields empty to use the automatic text, which stays up to date. Use **Use suggestion** to copy it in and
edit it. Setup steps for Google Search Console, Merchant Center and the buying guides are in `seo-guide.md`.
